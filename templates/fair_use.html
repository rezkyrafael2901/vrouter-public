Fair Use Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

VRouter provides access to AI models through a shared routing infrastructure. This Fair Use Policy ensures equitable access for all users and protects the stability of the platform.

Free Tier Limits

  • 10,000 tokens/day on core models (DeepSeek, Qwen, etc.)
  • Standard rate limiting applies (requests per minute)
  • No guaranteed SLA for free tier

Note: Free tier models include most open-weight models. Premium models (GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini Pro) may require a Pro subscription.

Pro Tier Limits

  • 1,000,000 tokens/day included
  • Access to all models including gated/premium
  • Priority routing and lower latency
  • Custom provider key support

What Is Not Allowed

  • Crypto mining / compute farming: Do not use the API for cryptocurrency mining, proof-of-work, or any computationally intensive non-AI tasks.
  • Abuse / spam generation: Bulk generating spam, phishing content, or deceptive material.
  • Automated scraping: Systematically extracting model capabilities or training data.
  • Resale without authorization: Reselling API access or building competing services without a Business agreement.
  • Rate limit circumvention: Creating multiple accounts to bypass rate limits.

Data and Privacy

  • We do not store your prompts or completions.
  • We collect anonymized metadata: model name, token counts, latency, and status codes.
  • Usage data is retained for 30 days for routing optimization, then purged.
  • We do not use your data to train models.

Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Temporary rate limiting or throttling
  • Account suspension pending review
  • Permanent account termination for severe or repeated violations

Questions?

If you have questions about what constitutes acceptable use, contact us at viresen16@gmail.com before proceeding.
